## What to check

- Does the portrait clip fit on your phone screen without scrolling?
- Do the controls work, and does the poster show before you press play?
- Does the caption and credit line match the image captions in other posts, like [what to bring to a rave](/blog/what-to-bring-to-a-rave/)?
