Kickback is a personal blog. I only measure what helps me write better posts.

## Visitor statistics

- **Vercel Web Analytics** counts page views without cookies and without storing your IP address. It runs for everyone.
- **Vercel Speed Insights** measures how fast pages load for visitors, also without cookies and without personal data. It runs for everyone.
- **Google Analytics** only runs if you click "Accept" in the cookie panel. It shows me which posts people read and where they came from. You can change your choice any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

## Newsletter

- If you sign up, you first get a confirmation email. Only after you click it is your email address stored by Resend, the email service that sends the newsletter. It is only used to send you new posts.
- Every email has an unsubscribe link that removes you right away.

## Questions

Message me via the links at the bottom of the page and I'll answer or delete your data on request.
