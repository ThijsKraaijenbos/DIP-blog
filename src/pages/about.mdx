Kickback is specifically made for rave fans within the uptempo and rawstyle scene. In this blog I share my own personal experiences, give honest and detailed reviews about raves and festivals I've been to, and I provide useful tips and guides that are not only useful for newcomers, but also for the scene veterans.

## Who I am

My name is Thijs Kraaijenbos. I myself am a big fan of the uptempo hardcore and the rawstyle scene. Especially DJs like Lekkerfaces, Toza, The Dark Horror, and Dead X. In late 2023 / early 2024 my brother started bringing me along to raves. After my first uptempo rave I very quickly fell in love with the music and started listening to it myself.

## How Kickback started

Whenever I wanted to know if a rave was worth the ticket, all I could find were aftermovies and lineup hype. I wanted honest reviews from someone who was actually there, and a place to hear what other ravers thought. So I started Kickback to share my own experiences and connect with people from the scene.

## How I review

I review only raves I've actually been to myself so I can fairly review them. Every review gets scored on several factors such as lineup, atmosphere, overall sound quality, location, facilities, etc.
